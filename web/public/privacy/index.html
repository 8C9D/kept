Kept Receipts — Privacy Policy

Kept Receipts is a small, unlisted receipt-keeping app built for a small business. It scans paper receipts, lets you confirm the details, and stores them so a year-end summary can go to an accountant. It is not a public product and has no ambition to be one.

What the app collects

  • Name and email address — provided by Sign in with Apple when you create your account (Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one; either is fine).
  • A user ID — the identifier Apple issues for your account, used to keep your receipts yours.
  • Financial information from your receipts — amounts and tax figures (such as HST).
  • Receipt photos — the images you scan.
  • Other receipt details — vendor names, dates, categories, and notes you enter.
  • Usage and interaction data — added August 28, 2026. Which screens and actions you use: for example, that a field was edited and how many times, that a suggested value was kept or changed, or that an export was started. This is never the values themselves — no vendor names, amounts, dates, categories, or notes are ever included, only that an action happened and which field it concerned.

How it is used

Your receipts and the account information above exist for one purpose: keeping your receipts and producing your year-end export. Usage and interaction data exists for a second, narrower purpose: seeing which parts of the app work well and which don't — for example, whether a suggested amount usually needs correcting. All of it is linked to your account, because receipts that are not linked to their owner are useless. None of it is used for tracking, advertising, or profiling — the app contains no third-party analytics and no advertising of any kind. Usage data stays first-party: it is read only by us, for improving the app, and is never combined with data from anywhere else. Your data is never sold or shared with anyone except as needed to store it (our database and file-storage providers).

Where it lives

Receipts and images are stored on servers we operate for this app. Each user sees only their own receipts. Receipt images travel directly from your phone to storage over encrypted connections.

Retention and deletion

Receipts are tax records, so they are kept for the retention period tax law expects (six years in Canada). Usage and interaction data is not a tax record and is kept for a much shorter window — up to 180 days. If you want your account and its data deleted, contact us and we will do it, subject to that legal retention where it applies; this deletes your receipts, images and usage data together.
